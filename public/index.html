How this model works

A full-range constant-product position starts with 50% ETH and 50% USDC by dollar value. USDC stays at $1. Ideal, frictionless arbitrage brings the pool price to the future market price.

For an initial investment V and price ratio r = future price / starting price, holding is worth V × (1 + r) / 2. The LP assets are worth V × √r before fee income. Initial ETH = V / (2 × starting price); after rebalancing, LP ETH = initial ETH / √r and LP USDC = initial USDC × √r.

Fee income is your assumed total for this position, added as separate USD cash at the end. It is held fixed across the comparison, does not compound, and is not inferred from market prices or trading volume. The model excludes gas, incentives, depegging, and concentrated liquidity. It shows endpoints, not a trading path.

Model version: cp50-v1. Shared links and CSV exports include the assumptions and model version so another person can reproduce the calculation.
